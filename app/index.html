Background music

Manage the folders RipeClips loads background tracks from.

Tracks are read directly from these folders on your Mac. Pick one from the ♪ Music button above a clip preview, set its volume, and it is mixed under your clip's audio on export. Supported formats: MP3, M4A, AAC, WAV, FLAC, OGG, OPUS.
